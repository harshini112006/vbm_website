Material Guide

Choosing the right material

A quick reference to the sizes we stock and where they are typically used.

TMT Bars

SizeTypically used for
8 mmStirrups, ties and light reinforcement
10 mmSlabs, lintels and sunshades
12 mmSlabs, beams and smaller columns
16 mmBeams and columns
20 – 25 mmColumns, footings and heavier structures

Bricks & Blocks

TypeTypically used for
4″ blocksPartition and internal walls
6″ blocksInternal walls and lighter external walls
8″ / 9″ blocksExternal and load-bearing walls
AAC blocksLighter walls with good heat insulation and faster laying
Wire cut red brickTraditional brickwork, in premium and standard quality

Sand & Jelly

MaterialTypically used for
M-SandConcrete and block work
P-SandFiner sand for plastering
¾″ jellyReinforced concrete: slabs, beams and columns
1½″ jellyPlain concrete and foundation beds
Filling sandBasement and site filling

General guidance only. Your structural engineer's drawings always come first, and we are happy to help you match them.
