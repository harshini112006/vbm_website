Questions

Frequently asked

Can't find your answer? Call us on 89256 81234.

Can I visit your store before ordering?

Yes, you're welcome to visit us at Potheri, Guduvancheri. Call ahead on 89256 81234 and we'll have what you need ready for you to see.

Which areas do you deliver to?

Guduvancheri and the surrounding villages, and the stretch from Maraimalai Nagar up to Chengalpattu. The full list is in the delivery areas above.

Is delivery free?

Delivery is free within a 5 km radius of Potheri for orders above 3 tonnes. For other orders, ask us about delivery when you request a quote.

Do you supply small residential orders?

Yes. We supply everything from small home builds to large projects, and tailor each order to what the site needs.

Which brands do you stock?

Cement from UltraTech, Dalmia, Ramco and Chettinad; TMT bars from TATA Tiscon, ARS, Isteel, JR, MCR and Sri Durga; AAC blocks from Renecon, Ekocon and JJM; and KAG tiles.

How do I get a quote?

Call 89256 81234 or 91769 97363, or send the form on this page with the materials and quantities you need.
