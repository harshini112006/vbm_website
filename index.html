About VBM

Two decades of trust. One complete mart.

Leon Steels Victorian Build Mart

After more than twenty years dealing in steel and cement for building construction, our business was incorporated in October 2020 to supply premium, trusted brands of TMT bars and cement across the region.

Today, as Victorian Build Mart, everything a build needs is available in one place, from sources we have tested and partners we trust. A new name, the same team and the same promise.

Our Journey

How we have grown

20+ years ago

Rooted in steel & cement

We began by dealing in steel and cement for building construction, earning the trust of local contractors and builders.

October 2020

A company is born

Incorporated as Leon Steels Private Limited to supply premium TMT bars and cement from brands such as ARS, Tata, UltraTech and Dalmia.

Growing range

Beyond the basics

The portfolio grew to solid, hollow, AAC and column blocks and precast beams, all from reputed manufacturers.

2026

Victorian Build Mart

A new name and a complete range: aggregates, bricks, tiles, sanitary ware, doors and windows, together with steel and cement.
