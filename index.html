Our Journey

How we have grown

20+ years ago

Rooted in steel & cement

We began by dealing in steel and cement for building construction, earning the trust of local contractors and builders.

October 2020

A company is born

The business was formally established to supply premium, trusted brands of TMT bars and cement — ARS, Tata, UltraTech and Dalmia — across the region.

Growing range

Beyond the basics

The portfolio grew to solid, hollow, AAC and column blocks and precast beams, all from reputed manufacturers.

Today

One complete mart

A complete range under one roof: aggregates, bricks, tiles, sanitary ware, doors and windows, together with steel and cement.
